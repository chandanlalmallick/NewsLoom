Frequently Asked Questions

What is News Loom?

News Loom is a static global news dashboard designed to organize stories across world affairs, science, climate, business, technology, sports and culture.

What topics does News Loom cover?

News Loom covers geopolitics and international affairs, science and space, climate and environment, economy and markets, technology and AI, sports and culture.

Can I search News Loom?

Yes. Use the search box to search the available article dataset by headline, category, location and article content.

Does News Loom provide live news?

The current GitHub Pages version uses a static editorial dataset. It should not be treated as a live wire-service or real-time news feed.

About News Loom

News Loom is a static GitHub Pages news-reading project designed to demonstrate a searchable global editorial dashboard.

The current 100-story dataset is an illustrative demonstration dataset rather than a live feed. For real-world publication, stories should be replaced or connected to verified reporting and primary sources.
